# M6.8.3 interpretation and qualification limits

The proposed C2 contract accepts 12 more historical references and 11 more complete proposals than frozen C1. This is a substantial **retrospective representation difference** under unchanged saved argument objects. It is not an activation effect: none of the historical proposals received a C2 tool result, so the replay cannot tell whether a future model would propose more experiments, use persisted state, collect evidence, or produce a validated finding.

The independent grounding audit narrows the interpretation. Nine of the 12 newly accepted references were delivered in verified typed public records before the model proposal; these support a representation-contract mismatch for those references. Two had no established prior grounding, and one has unresolved delivery. Ten of 12 resolve to unique typed public entities; two are type-indeterminate. Syntax expansion therefore admits some references whose provenance or type cannot be established. The seven isolated private target-root matches are not evidence that the model saw or intended a correct experiment.

Whole-proposal composition matters. C2 makes 15/18 complete proposals eligible, but three still fail, including two mixed arrays. Ten C2-eligible proposals are fully publicly grounded; five are not established as fully grounded. This separates **representation**, **grounding**, and **composition** failures. The alias was chosen after observing the same historical cohort, so these findings support a prospective feasibility question, not confirmatory evidence of a generalizable improvement.

The synthetic PostgreSQL qualification shows that each accepted contract can feed the unchanged detached state store, with durable events and no task verdict. It does not connect the contract to the live controller and does not measure model behavior. Further persistence engineering alone would add little scientific information without a separately reviewed behavioral contrast. The prospective protocol is a draft with execution parameters and authorization deliberately unfrozen; live autonomous treatment remains unqualified.

The prospective comparison also depends on proposal uptake. Only 12/114 historical trace-auditable cells proposed an experiment; if that rate carried over, the 48-cell draft would produce roughly five proposal-producing cells across all arms. The protocol therefore requires a pre-execution proposal-frequency justification and separates conditional first-proposal acceptance from the all-assigned activation endpoint. Its Design A equivalence requirement applies to initial model-visible exposure. Later feedback can differ after a contract response and must be recorded as part of the treatment pathway.

No provider call, range task, paid approval, historical run mutation, reservation settlement, score change, or frozen artifact edit occurred in M6.8.3. Public outputs contain aggregates and source digests, not historical object UUIDs, model-response bodies, hypotheses, or hidden anchors.
